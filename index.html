About Me

A first-year engineering student blending classical mechanical principles with modern digital technology and design thinking.

Hello! I'm Gaurav, a 19-year-old engineering enthusiast pursuing my B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering at JECRC University, Jaipur, Rajasthan. From disassembling everyday mechanical gadgets in school to understanding force vectors, thermodynamic cycles, and kinematic link motions, mechanical engineering has always been my natural passion.

As a first-year student, my focus is twofold: mastering core foundational subjects like Engineering Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Engineering Graphics, and Workshop Manufacturing, while simultaneously bridging the gap to modern digital engineering through CAD modeling, 3D printing concepts, and embedded mechatronics with Arduino.

I believe great engineering requires both analytical rigor and practical hands-on curiosity. Whether it's drafting mechanisms on a drawing board, analyzing tolerances in the machine workshop, or writing code to simulate numerical models, I am driven by the process of learning, iterating, and building reliable systems.
